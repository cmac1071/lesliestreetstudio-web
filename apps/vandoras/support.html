About Vandoras

Vandoras is an interactive graphic novel for iOS. It is a single purchase from the App Store — no ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases. Everything in the game is available from the moment you install it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase Vandoras?

Vandoras is available on the App Store as a one-time purchase. There are no in-app purchases and no subscription fees.

Is there anything to buy inside the app?

No. The App Store purchase is the only transaction associated with Vandoras. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, and no premium content behind additional paywalls.

How long does the game take to complete?

A single playthrough takes approximately 11 hours. Vandoras has three distinct endings — each requires meaningful choices throughout the story that push the narrative in different directions. Reaching all three endings requires replaying the game.

Can I replay chapters or make different choices?

Details on chapter replay and save management will be documented here when the game launches.

Where is my progress saved?

All save data is stored locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted to a server or shared with any third party.

Is there sound or music?

Yes. Vandoras has an original score and synthesized voice narration. Ensure your device is not in silent mode for the full experience. Audio settings will be described here at launch.

What devices are supported?

Vandoras is designed for iPhone running iOS. Minimum iOS version and device requirements will be listed on the App Store page at launch.
