Vandoras

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 25, 2026

Vandoras is developed by Leslie Street Studio LLC. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data Collection

Vandoras does not collect any personal information. The app does not require an account, registration, or any user-provided data to function.

Data Storage

All save data — including story progress, choices made, and chapter completion — is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to the developer, any server, or any third party. Deleting the app removes all stored data.

Advertising

Vandoras contains no advertising of any kind. No ad networks are integrated into the app.

In-App Purchases

Vandoras has no in-app purchases. The App Store purchase is the only transaction associated with the app. Purchase transactions are processed entirely by Apple. The developer does not receive or store any payment information.

Analytics and Tracking

The app does not use any first-party analytics, crash reporting, or tracking services. The app does not collect device identifiers, usage statistics, or location information.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

Updates to this privacy policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy: support@lesliestreetstudio.com
