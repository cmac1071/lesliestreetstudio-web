The Story

A craftsman in a pre-industrial city opens a fouled lock and something changes. Not the lock. Him.

Text-primary interactive puzzle novel for iOS. Choice-driven narrative with puzzle interludes. Three factions, three endings, one character whose gift will either serve the world or unmake part of it.

Cosmic order is not self-sustaining. It is maintained. Three factions want Van for different reasons — and only one of them is honest about what the work will cost him.

Sparse illustration, original score, synthesized voice narration. The game is the story, not the puzzles. Van's register is plain and precise, and so is the game's.

Concordance. Ledger. Unwinding.

One playthrough won't show you all of it.

The Puzzles

Three languages. One vocabulary.

01

Picking Locks

Van's oldest vocabulary. Tap, drag, set. But these pins have learned to resist — and the mechanism he's working knows who's asking.

Tap · Drag · Tension

02

Reassembling Gear Trains

Ratios, flows, configurations. Not every gear belongs in every arrangement. Find the routing that holds — and understand what it means when one doesn't.

Rotate · Connect · Configure

03

Aligning Glyphwork

The heaviest language. Sixteen glyphs, relational constraints, one valid configuration. You don't always know which answer you're giving until the frame closes.

Glyphs · Constraints · Order
Three Acts

The story you play.

I

The Awakening

A routine commission. A lockbox that responds differently than it should. Van opens something he can't close. Three factions arrive — each with a different story about what he is and what he owes them.

Ch 1–3 · Harrowmere · Tutorial Puzzles
II

The Reckoning

Six chapters across failing mines, besieged keeps, and Concordance archives. Van helps people whose problems resist conventional solutions — and learns that his gift has been tracked for seven generations. At the end, he chooses.

Ch 4–9 · Six Locations · Faction Choices
III

The Consequence

Three separate stories. Three distinct endings. One shared crisis at Vael Moren — an ancient stability node failing on its own schedule — before the tracks diverge into conclusions that don't resemble each other.

Ch 10–12 · Three Tracks · Three Endings
Three Endings

Your choices are Van's choices.

Vandoras has no single ending. Every conversation shifts where your loyalty seems to lie, and the choices you make across all twelve chapters carry you toward one of three very different conclusions — different confrontations, different costs, different endings entirely.

The Concordance

The old order, maintained.

The Concordance wants the old order maintained, quietly, by people who understand what happens when it isn't. They will court Van. They won't lie outright.

Order · Continuity · Quiet Authority

The Ledger

The same mechanism, different owners.

The Ledger wants the same mechanism working for different owners, and has never much cared what it costs anyone else. They will court Van. They won't lie outright.

Leverage · Ambition · Cost

The Unwinding

Stopped, for good.

The Unwinding wants it stopped for good, and has its own reasons for believing that stopping it is mercy. They will court Van. They won't lie outright.

Conviction · Mercy · Reckoning
